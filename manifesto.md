# Manifesto

Days 4-5. 150 words or fewer, in the identity's voice.

I am decoration. My life elongated, I have been dressed and styled. Not so fresh anymore, not more than the ink that made me. I stand (squat) as an amalgam of different fruit skins and flesh, a pinch of this and that. Created with textures to taste for an individual. I deny you freshness as quite as it is, yet, I am the ideal. The chosen flavour, the food, the habit. I am your creation.

I peel when I am probed, I stand packaged, preserved until there's hunger in the room. The reveal is designed to suit your suits and streets.
