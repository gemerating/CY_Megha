# Codex metaphor log

Day 3. Five possible computational metaphors for Cabble. The third was chosen for development.

## Five metaphors

1. **A customized interface** — Cabble's textures and reveal are designed around a particular person's needs and preferences.
2. **A read-only file** — Cabble is held in a preserved, fixed form, like an ideal image that cannot become fresh again.
3. **A query that changes the object** — each probe brings an answer or peeling response, changing Cabble and the person asking.
4. **A cached image** — Cabble holds an ideal version of fruit after its freshness has passed, like a stored image standing in for something living.
5. **A recommendation algorithm** — Cabble reflects what a person is expected to want while also shaping that desire.

## The one kept

**A query that changes the object.** This was chosen because Cabble's response can unfold over time: it rests, performs when touched, lifted, or probed, and the response undoes itself when probing stops. Its encounters change Cabble and its creator, even though the change does not remain as a permanent mark.

## What the others got wrong


- **Customized interface:** what data would I be extracting from an individual from whom the website is designed? how much time is needed and what decides does the interface serve as? 
- **Read-only file:** very interesting but it only focuses on the perishable aspect of the fruit.
- **Cached image:** I don't fully understand this. 
- **Recommendation algorithm:** Wouldn't want to get too much data from the person trying to engage.
