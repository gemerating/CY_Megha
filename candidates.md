# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1. Cabble (flying fruit)
From the future, positioned in Bengaluru. The fruit is 3D printed and the recipe and print files for regeneration act as a seed. They can either be put on a website  or as an SD card inside the fruit.

## 2. Hephy (balloon animal)
From the present, positioned in Delhi, is the last art piece left for sale at the crossroads.

## 3. Tzins (plastiglomerate carved sculpture/assemblage)
From the future, it is telling of our Anthropocene and the shape future fossils could take.
